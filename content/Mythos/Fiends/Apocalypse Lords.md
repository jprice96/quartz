
## Briphokh, the Rotten
An ancient daemon who wanders the stretches of his domain endlessly, followed by various hangers on seeking to scrounge something from his overwhelming presence without it consuming them entirely. Theologians liken him to dying of thirst while afloat in an ocean of brine, he represents a duality of both excess and scarcity, to have so much and yet have it all be rotten or worthless. Early goblin stories claim that [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Kanna|Kanna]] was inspired by an encounter with Briphokh, that he consumes as much as he can for he knows it will eventually come to rot otherwise. While this Lord doesn't actively dissuade others from following him, he also doesn't seem to have any desire to recruit other fiends to his influence.
## Novimu, the Blue Princess
A devil who rules over an infernal court vying for her fleeting attention, she is a favorite among [[Bestiary/Demons#Immitis|immitis]] demons who often compete for her fleeting favor. She represents one of the many apocalypses in which mortals create their own doom, one in which mortals kill each other not out of violence or necessity, but for sport. Extremist sects of [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Jirem|Jirem]] sometimes praise her as the game master of life, the one who will be there to tell them they have won for they are the last mortal standing. In the mean time she seems to primarily involve herself in stirring up the politics of both Infernum and the Material Realm, driving both revolutionary coups and tyrannical strangleholds depending on what seems to be little more than whim.
## Python, the Ensnaring
An elusive fiend, it is unknown if they are a daemon or something else, fitting their title as the Lord of Secrets. They have a number of followers, although such fiends don't announce themselves as such, instead acting as eyes and ears throughout Infernum on behalf of their lord. Python understands the power of secrets, both in keeping them and in spilling them to the right people at the right times (or rather the wrong people at the wrong times, to cause maximum damage). As such, many seek out the serpentine fiend in order to acquire information that they might not acquire from elsewhere, especially as they tend to be more willing to slip forbidden knowledge than [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Jirem|Jirem]].
## Vivellom, the All-Mother
A daemon queen who leads hordes of lesser monstrosities to do her bidding, all her children that she births knowing they will die before her. Some claim she is the mother of all daemons, or at least most of them, although the veracity of such claims is unclear. She represents the duality of infertility and overpopulation, it is said by some that children born with physical deformities were touched by one of Vivellom's daemons while in the womb. Her children all do her bidding with mindless loyalty, and it seems that she can control in some manner the nature of the children she births as she needs them.
