From the northern edges of the [[Geography/Natural/Landforms#Cliffs of Shrieking Clouds|Cliffs of Shrieking Clouds]], to the forking of the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Ming River|Ming River]], the Bylian tribe roam - a terror to any foolish enough to travel that far north. Practitioners of traditions deemed needlessly cruel or barbaric, even by Vurnamese standards, they are a fiercely proud and isolated people. Much of their regular contact with outsiders involve skirmishes with the [[Geography/Vurnam/Tribes/Tzusa|Tzusa tribe]] along the south of their territory, or terrifying supernatural clashes with the [[Culture/List of Factions#Protarchik Coven|Coven of the Boundless]] on the coast. Few others will approach them, turning around with great haste when the spot their painted white faces from afar in the northern wilds.
