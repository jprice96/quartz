Along the southern branches of the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Ming River|Ming River]], the Tzusa or the Clan of the Yellow Lotus reside. A relatively peaceful tribe, the Tzusa primarily forage over hunting, and are experts at turning the various vegetation of the area into edible goods. Much of the local vegetation can be dangerous if not properly prepared, so they take the alchemical and culinary arts seriously, with such learned individuals being held in esteem. They have a number of permanent settlements, although they are often deliberately obfuscated and the surrounding natural pathways lie trapped and disorienting. They have a history of conflict with the [[Geography/Vurnam/Tribes/Bylian|Bylian]] tribe in the north, leading them to be wary of outsiders, but they would often prefer to avoid conflict than fight outright. Just as many of the dangerous beasts of the region do, when the Tzusa strike they often do so from hiding and with lethal intent.
