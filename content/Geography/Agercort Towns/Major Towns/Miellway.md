The second largest town in [[Geography/Agercort/Agercort|Agercort]], Miellway is the home of the Cathedral of Morning Lights, the Arcenciel Tower, and Castle Resplendence.

## Cathedral of Morning Lights
A major center of worship for [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé|Panismé]], many clerics and devout followers across Agercort make a pilgrimage to Miellway to either study at the Cathedral or simple to gaze upon its beauty. It is renowned for its dazzling array of windows made not from stained glass but from finely cut and polished crystal.
## Arcenciel Tower
The highest point in Miellway, Arcenciel (ark-in-seyul) Tower was erected in 2E 212 under the direction of the wizard Philippe the Stargazer. It is said that Philippe spent near every coin he had on the construction of the tower (and that he faced great opposition from the supposedly jealous Lord Exuberance). Made from stone quarried to the south, it stands at just 1,100ft tall and is capped with a substance the wizard called 'sunsilver' which has the appearance of polished silver yet has stood untarnished upon the tower without maintenance for centuries. The tower's purpose was to further Philippe's study of the stars which he believed could be used to augment or amplify arcane rituals in the same way some witch covens depend upon the lunar cycle. While [[Culture/People/Sophia Pistis|Sophia Pistis]] supposedly visited the Tower on two separate occasions, once during Philippe's life and again in 2E 550, the merits of Philippe's work are unclear as after his death the tower has seen little use. In 2E 313, a decade after Philippe's death and just over a century after its construction, the Tower was claimed by a group of scholars who were able to produce a will of Philippe's. This group, now called the Academy of Cosmosis, continue to maintain and use the Tower to this day as a library, arcane laboratory, and school for those seeking to learn the arcane.
## Castle Resplendence
Home to the Exuberance family for generations, they are one of the oldest noblesse families in all Agercort.
