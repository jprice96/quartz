A small town of around 1400 people on the eastern shore of [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Aventure Lakes|Grand Aventure]], Heartlake's primary industry is [[Culture/Crops and Agriculture#Leshy-Hearts|leshy-hearts]]. A number or orchards span the area around the town, and there is much work following harvest time at the end of autumn and throughout the winter in which the fruits are turned into preserves, dried, or fermented into the popular greenwine cider. They also act as a central market for many of the farmsteads heading out north and east, and much trade passes through on it's way to and from [[Geography/Agercort/Major Towns/Miellway|Miellway]] ensuring relatively well supplied markets and stores for a town of this size.
## Verdant Family
The prime noblesse family of Heartlake are the Verdants. Taking the role of head of house in 3E 161 was Lord Jeremy Verdant after his mother passed leaving the title to him despite there being an older brother, Tomothy. The forsaken son had run off to the northern border years prior with little regard for his home or mother, and no one has really heard from Tomothy in over a decade. As current Lord Verdant, Jeremy oversees the tithing of the orchards, trade negotiations with merchants passing through on the route to and from Miellway, as well as maintaining good relations with the noblesse of Miellway, the [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Heartlake#Chapel of the Sweet|local chapel]] and the nearby [[Geography/Agercort/Other Locations/Wildbloom Monastery|Wildbloom Monastery]]. He also cares for his younger sister, Avalon, who often acts as just one more burden placed on the young lord's shoulders given her disregard for propriety.
## Verdant Guard
Employed by the Verdant family, the Verdant Guard maintain order in Heartlake and the surroundings. A small corp of about 20 permanent guards who patrol the region and maintain regular training and equipment, they have a barracks attached to the Verdant Manor complex although most maintain personal residences with family as well. They wear dark green tabards with the Verdant symbol of a tree emblazoned in white. 
## Chapel of the Sweet
The local chapel in town is dedicated to [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé|Panismé]], as well as dedicated shrines to the muse [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé#Erte, Muse of Desire|Erte]] and to Ellyglen, a fae figure of local importance said to watch over the orchards. It is here that much of the town's greenwine is produced, as the chapel owns a small warehouse as part of its compound which it uses to house the fermentation equipment as well as any excess stock before it is distributed or shipped either north or south.
## The Next Drink
A tavern and inn more popular among the locals than many of those travelling through on trade or business, the place is run by a man called Alois who inherited it from the late Lady Verdant. The scandal involved in Alois' inheritance of the tavern meant that many of the travelling merchants who used to pass through began to look for other lodgings, which meant business wasn't doing too well until Alois was able to attract more local patronage.
## Bellbound Corner
A popular inn amongst travelers and traders passing through Heartlake, it is run by a pair of cousins - Gregory and Charles - who started the place a few years ago. For starting from scratch they have made quite the success for themselves, although there are some lingering questions about how exactly the pair afforded such a venture given neither come from wealth. The Bellbound Corner is a decently sized inn with dozens of rooms, a fully equipped stable and wagon yard, even a small bathhouse for patrons. The name comes from the tiny copper bells that seem to hang in odd corners and nooks all around the place, a superstition from the owners who claim it wards of bad luck.
## Greentongue Guild
A historical guild present in Heartlake for centuries, the Greentongue guild were once the liaisons between the mortals of Heartlake and the local fae. During the [[Culture/History/Scarlett War|Scarlett War]] their importance declined, and so by the Third Era they have become more a group of enthusiasts than anyone of real importance. The apparent retreat of the fae from the region has not swayed their efforts, and many of the remaining members pass on the traditions of the sylvan tongue and fae lore and traditions. They are sometimes at odds with the nearby [[Geography/Agercort/Other Locations/Wildbloom Monastery|Wildbloom Monastery]] who disagree with some of their practices, but mostly the two groups just avoid each other. Most of the townsfolk these days see the guild as little more than a bit of fun, although there are still some who adhere to the guilds advice whether due to superstition or just traditions.
## Mint Condition
A small apothecary shop with an attached clinic for those in need of medical attention, although most in need seek out the [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Heartlake#Chapel of the Sweet|Chapel]] first. Run by a halfling alchemist called Blake, she collects all manner of herbs and flower which she methodically dries and stores in great shelves. While she can't really help with a stab wound, if she knows a disease or condition has an alchemical treatment she can competently produce and administer such a thing. She is frankly more of a collector and merchant than a medic though, as she will gladly tell you herself. She lives in a small apartment above her store and doesn't get out much, which has led to some thinking of her as a bit of a shut-in.
## Market Square
Towards the eastern edge of town sits a great square in which many farmers from the local region will come to hawk their remaining crops after harvest. In the off-seasons the square is a bit emptier, but there are always a couple of hunters, fishers, artisans, or travelling merchants setting up at a stall to sell something. Having so many trading caravans pass through on their way to Miellway means that there is always the chance of some exotic bargain.
## Avcott & Lincoln Legal
A small office above some of the other general shopfronts in the middle of town, run by Jean Avcott and her halfling associate Lionel Lincoln, the two offer legal and bookkeeping services. Given the trade that comes through town, they do well enough to keep the office open, but most of their work has historically been for the Verdant family. They do have a number of records in their office going back a couple of generations of the family.
## Waterview Wheel
The primary tailor in town, Suzanna runs the workshop employing a half dozen people to spin wool, strip leather, and sew clothing of all sorts for the people of Heartlake. Down by the lakeside, she often receives shipments of textiles and dyes from upriver. While most people view her as a businesswoman, her aunt Cora is less well-liked. The old woman will often slip in and try and 'help' with the work - Suzanna tries to keep her out and most of the time is able to catch her before anything goes too awry.
