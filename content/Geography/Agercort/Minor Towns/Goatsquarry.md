A limestone quarry that ships to many of the larger settlements along the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Jelrye River|Jelrye]]. It was once known as "Ghostquarry", but was officially changed after persistent campaigning by the Gesorn family who have claimed nobility over the town since before the formation of [[Geography/Agercort/Agercort|Agercort]] at the start of the Second Era. The Gesorn family have the goat as a sort of familial mascot, there is goat iconography throughout the town - although very few actual goats.
