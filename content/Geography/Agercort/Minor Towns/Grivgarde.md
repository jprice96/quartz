
A day south of [[Geography/Agercort/Major Towns/Joytham|Joytham]], Grivgarde is renowned as a retreat for the noblesse and wealthy foreigners. Home to the a thriving pleasure district in which all manner of purveyors offer blessings to [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé#Grivois, Muse of Pleasure|Grivois]], it strikes an uneasy balance between sleaze and sophistication as befitting its patron muse.

## Lemacreau Family
Influence along the dock fronts, they own many of the brothels from the whorehouses on the riverside to the burlesque establishments near the gardens

## Church of Felicity
Dedicated to [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé|Panismé]] and [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Panismé#Grivois, Muse of Pleasure|Grivois]], they are also connected to many of the more esoteric substances available in Grivgarde and have a lot of connections with the Blue Apothecaries

## Juborsé Family
Rumors surrounding the Juborsé family fortunes abound, as they profit from many of the gambling dens and game houses in Grivgarde - as well as being the ones who primarily employ the town guards

## Sagourney Family
With access to the finest nuts, fruits, meats, and cheeses available to the Agercort noblesse, the Sagourney family has invested in decadence. For them quality is better than quantity - although for a high enough price you can have both - eating in one of their parlors is far beyond the financial reach of most.
