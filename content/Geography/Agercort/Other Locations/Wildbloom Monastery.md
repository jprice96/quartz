Built between the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Aventure Lakes|Aventure Lakes]] in 2E 118 by a group of travelling monks under the leadership of a human guru called Master Vozhu, the Monastery has stood as a separate community since then despite multiple attempts to integrate them into the nearby [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Heartlake|Heartlake]]. The surrounding area is filled with a spread of wildflowers which the monks maintain in conjunction with their beekeeping. The honey, wax, and floral goods they collect are often sold in [[Geography/Agercort/Major Towns/Miellway|Miellway]] in exchange for other goods. Outside of such trading trips, the monks remain relatively reclusive in their monastery claiming they practice a form of tranquility that would be disrupted by cosmopolitan influences.

The monks who live within the monastery practice a form of martial arts and meditation described by scholars as the way of the twisting petal. Their original leader Vozhu taught them that they should live life as a wildflower - unobtrusive and yet resilient. Many of their philosophies and practices include an acknowledgement or deference to [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Lingzul|Lingzul]] New initiates to the order must study for at least two years, after which they may be inducted and are granted a new name taken from a flower just as Vozhu did. They experienced a good deal of distrust during the [[Culture/History/Scarlett War|Scarlett War]], particularly in the later years, due to their insistence on abstaining from the conflict.
