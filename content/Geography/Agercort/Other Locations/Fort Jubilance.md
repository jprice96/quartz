
Situated within the middle of [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Shrieker's Fen|Shrieker's Fen]], Fort Jubilance is home to the Order of the Cat's Lily and was erected in 2E 320 by the founders of the Order to "bring Panismé's jubilance during these dark days". It sits on the road through the marshy ground between [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Passionmarsh|Passionmarsh]] and [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Wegmarket|Wegmarket]], built using moorstone from [[Geography/Agercort/Minor Towns/Goldcross|Goldcross]].

## Order of the Cat's Lily
Founded during the early years of the [[Culture/History/Scarlett War|Scarlett War]], the Order sought not to go and fight for the freedoms of the oppressed on the front lines, but to fight for the peoples of [[Geography/Agercort/Major Towns/Joytham|Joytham]]. After an entire generation had been sent off to fight, the stability of [[Geography/Agercort/Agercort|Agercort]] had begun to falter - banditry, undue taxation, persecution, the people of the land left behind no longer had many of the young and valiant who would normally defend them. The Order was formed with the goal of preserving the safety and happiness of the people left at home, and did so until the War finally ended. Since then the Order has been somewhat lost, there is not as much need for them and there are some who now see them as a relic of the past, others who see them as fools seeking to play knight.
