
## Soothfast Order
An order of knights who reside at Griffins' Keep, they have pledged themselves to [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Tenesia#Iverita, Angel of Truth|Iverita]], the Angel of Truth. Following the philosophies of [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Tenesia|Tenesia]], they hold the Angel as their ideal and believe honesty to be the primary virtue. They have sworn oaths to never lie or deceive, which they take incredibly seriously and results in a manner that can come across as off-putting to outsiders - especially the local noblesse. It does not help that they refuse to swear fealty to the crown of Agercort. The general populace of the region are happy to have the Order however, as despite their abrasiveness they are generally seen as champions of the downtrodden. They often roam the [[Geography/Natural/Landforms#Æmtiche Moors|Æmtiche Moors]] and the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Marshswallow River|Marshswallow River]] aiding those who ask for their help, whether it be fending off bandits and wild beasts, tending to the sick or injured, or helping to douse flames or raise barns.

The most notable aspect of the Order are their mounts. All of the full knights of the Order have a griffin companion - indeed it is a criterion to become a full knight. They believe that the griffin will only aid an applicant who is truly honest, and should a knight every break their vows their griffin will know and tear them to pieces.
