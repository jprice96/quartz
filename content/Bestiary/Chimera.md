
## Griffon
![[Bestiary/Images/Chimera_Griffon.png]]
One of the most well known kinds of chimera, griffons seem to be found nesting around the cliffs and mountains of [[Geography/Agercort homepage|Agercort homepage]], although they can roam a good many miles around on the search for food. Often heard before they are seen, they announce their arrival with a piercing shriek as they dive to pluck smaller animals from the earth, razor sharp talons ripping them as they lift them up to be taken elsewhere. Despite their terrifying appearance, there are some groups such as the Soothfast Order who have managed to tame the creatures, using them as flying mounts.


## Peryton
![Peryton](Bestiary/Images/Chimera_Peryton.png)
A lesser form of chimera, the peryton is often found in the foothills and cliffs of [[Geography/Mallerand homepage|Mallerand homepage]], although lone individuals have been spotted elsewhere. Preferring rocky precipices and tall trees, they will stalk prey sometimes for days on end before striking from above. When they do, they will often go right for the heart, ripping it out of the chest. Why they do this isn't entirely clear, but it seems to be the only part of the body that is actually eaten.

Some daring individuals attempt to train perytons, they do possess sufficient intellect but they are incredibly antisocial, typically considering other creatures as either prey or competition. Rarely however a peryton will recognise another creature, be it another peryton or a trainer, as an acceptable leader. In these cases the peryton serves ceaselessly and is a vicious bloodhound, apparently able to "smell a target's shadow".
