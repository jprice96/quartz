
## Akuwal
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Akuwal.png]]
Infamous amongst demons are the akawal, demons of gluttony born from the various sins of cannibalism. While there are living mortals who engage in the foul acts of cannibalism, most akawal are spawned from the actions of the undead who consume the flesh, blood, or even essence of their still living peers. The akawal itself laps at the living energies of those within their presence, simply dying in their presence is enough to cause unholy resurrection and it is believed by many in the [[Culture/Factions/Whitesun Lodge|Whitesun Lodge]] that sudden outbreaks of undeath are caused by the summoning of an akawal demon.
## Axeye
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Axeye.png]]
Lesser demons of wrath, axeye despise all things beautiful or artistic. Should they encounter anything or anyone who they perceive to be such they will fly into a destructive rage causing them to smash and rip and tear until they have ruined everything around them. While in this state they are quite violent and difficult to control, making them difficult tools to use and easy enough to bait into foolish mistakes. Despite this they are common, born from the self-loathing of mortals who feel their peers are succeeding while they shamefully struggle and fail.

## Chitesk

Powerful demons of envy who lurk on the fringes of fiendish courts and are sometimes sent to the Material Realm to wreak havoc, chitesk twist and contort the truth in order to manipulate others. They revel in the satisfaction of gaslighting others and setting them up for failure, and the further the fall from grace the more satisfying it is to the chitesk. Their multitude of tails both allow them exceptional senses as well as possessing the ability to mentally manipulate mortals by planting suggestions or false memories into their minds with a single touch.

## Iliui
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Iliui.png]]
Similar in appearance to a gigantic spider, an iliui is a demon of lust for intoxicants who can produce all manner of addictive and destructive concoctions from mixtures of their own bodily fluids. While they can take the form of a mortal, the effect is purely an illusion resulting in uncanny or inexplicable flaws in their disguise. Typically the mortals who are dealing with them are either already intoxicated or desperate for something to feed their addictions and so are not often the most discerning. Iliui often use hallucinogenic intoxicants or the threat of withholding their substances to ensure the obedience of their mortal victims. Amongst other fiends they are able to provide all kinds of effective stimulants, although such drugs are often dangerous if used repeatedly.
## Immitis
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Immitis.png]]
Demons born from vanity and ego, and immitis is obsessed with appearances, often in the form of beauty but not always. Whatever the avenue, they seek to be the most notable, the highest ranked, the most impressive, and they often have no problems with putting others down as a way to further elevate their status. The runes they have tattooed onto their body not only allow them to create complex illusions to change their appearance however they wish, but they also carry subtle enchantments that cause those who look upon them to see them with an extra air of awe and glamour. While still more physically competent than the average mortal, an immitis prefers to have underlings do the messy work, often enchanting their mortal admirers into complete subjugation.

## Joku
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Joku.png]]
Massive demons of gluttony and envy, their hulking appearance belies their expansive intellect and knowledge. Joku demons come from an obsessive desire to collect and hoard both material wealth and knowledge, both typically of a magical nature. They go to great lengths to both acquire (or steal) new knowledge while protecting their own hoard from being stolen by others. Many find themselves in association with [[Mythos/The Mortal Gods/Jirem|Jirem]], while others work as administrators or advisors for high ranking [[Mythos/Fiends/Devils|devils]] or even [[Mythos/Fiends/Apocalypse Lords|apocalypse lords]]. Should they find something they want that they are unable to take by force, they may be willing to trade for it, but they are just as likely to try and destroy the offending riches so that none can possess it if they cannot.

## Kiixni
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Kiixni.png]]
Lesser envy demons, kiixni can sometimes be confused with ghosts as they are typically incorporeal and are often imperceptible to mortals when weak. Born from the desire to be someone else, kiixni stalk and watch a mortal from the shadows often for weeks or months. Eventually they find the perfect moment and physically possess their target, controlling them from within while the victim watches on in terror. At first the kiixni will try and imitate out the victim's life but soon they begin to become bolder and more erratic as their own fiendish energy begins to warp the mortal body until eventually the kiixni is expelled through some sort of external intervention or they accidentally kill their mortal host with their reckless behaviour.

## Kipano
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Kipano.png]]
Kipano demons are creatures of such pride that they don't seem capable of acknowledging even other fiends as actual beings. Born from the sins of enslavement, they treat everyone else besides themselves as not much more than dumb beasts if not outright objects. To that end they often 'collect' mortals and lesser fiends as slaves using a combination of both physical force and ritualistic domination. Once someone has been claimed by a kipano the only way to free them is to kill the demon, a difficult feat to accomplish while keeping their slaves alive.
## Lateki
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Lateki.png]]
Subservient and sniveling, lateki demons are a lesser kind of sloth demon spawned from those unwilling to accept responsibility. They willingly throw themselves to the whims of stronger fiends in order to relieve themselves of the burden of free will - crippled by having to make choices and then to take responsibility for those choices leave them as obedient slaves suited for tasks requiring little to no input or problem solving. Many mages willing to traffic with infernal entities will conjure and bind lateki to acts as disposable minions, mistaking their subservience for loyalty. A lateki is not loyal, it will obey whoever it believes to be the strongest and is quick to change masters when encountering a more intimidating option.

## Lenopaxi
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Lenopaxi.png]]
Some of the most unusual of demonkind, lenopaxi are vanity demons who are unaware of their fiendish nature but instead believe themselves to be celestials. They disguise themselves as mortals, often in positions of political or religious power, and play the role of devout do-gooder and pillar of the community. Unfortunately they are born from the sins of vapid virtue signaling and lack any real substance, but they often play the part so well that they end up causing harm. The more people come to depend upon them, the more they are let down yet often are unable to see that it is the lenopaxi's negligence that has caused their problems. Even more dangerous, sometimes they garner enough goodwill and renown to meet an actual celestial. In most cases even a lesser celestial would immediately recognize a disguised fiend, but a lenopaxi's vanity is such that all but the most powerful of celestials are unable to see through them. To a celestial a lenopaxi's touch is like poison, often they succumb before they even realize what has happened to them, thanking the lenopaxi for nursing them through this terrible tragedy only to die in their arms.

## Najket
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Najket.png]]
While many demons seek to climb the power structure of Infernum, najket demons however are some of the most powerful of the pride demons unwilling to submit to anyone except perhaps an [[Mythos/Fiends/Apocalypse Lords|apocalypse lord]]. They are born from the sins of those who sacrificed their values and morals to achieve power and elevation, and the quickly amass a horde of fiends to serve beneath them eager to share in a glimpse of the najket's success (and hoping they are not sacrificed in the process). Following the [[Culture/History/Scarlett War|Scarlett War]] the number of najket demons swelled, causing a great deal of infighting on Infernum as the war waged in the Material Realm.

## Pachelli
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Pachelli.png]]
Powerful demons often accompanied by a small group of victims for them to slowly feed upon, pachelli are despair demons born from those who weren't content to simply be miserable but to constantly inflict their doubts and despair on all those around them. There are many who associate pachelli with [[Mythos/The Three Terrors#Kakova, Terror of Malice|Kakova]] for the reason that they are gifted with a metaphorical lead tongue which they use to whisper terrible and painful words to others, a constant tide of despair that often leaves them too uncaring to put up any resistance to the pachelli's machinations. Unlike many demons though pachelli don't seek to kill mortals outright, instead they keep them alive to slowly feed upon their blood - it is unclear whether this is simply to foster their despair or if the demon gains some pleasure or power from the feeding.
## Sokit
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Sokit.png]]
Lumbering creatures that ooze filth and pus that poisons whatever it touches, sokit are sloth demons produced from an excess of lethargy and laziness to the point of becoming degraded and diseased. They loathe moving, often preferring to laze in pools of liquid that soothe their boils and blisters. Should they need to move they have mastered the ability to teleport rather than go through the effort of actually moving their body. They are rarely summoned deliberately to the Material Realm, nor do they have much reason to go on their own.

## Tajolis
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Tajolis.png]]
Rarely alone, tajolis demons are wrathful and hateful creatures who look for any opportunity to pick a fight with mortals. In their eyes they are better than mortals, who are but worms squirming in the dirt beneath them. Supposedly the product of the various prejudices and discriminations of mortals against each other, tajolis demons find any example of mortal virtue an offense and will brutalize and bully such examples until they conform with the squalor and depravity expected of them. The more tajolis gang together the more powerful the collective becomes.

## Teyotil
![[Bestiary/Images/Demon_Teyotil.png]]
The archetypal demon of lust, teyotil demons are temptresses who often appear as mortals in the Material Realm. Spawned from a lust for attention, they fawn over their targets with physical affection, rapt admiration, verbal adulation, whatever kind of attention it is that the person so desperately wants. In doing so they often both garner attention back for themselves, as well as being able to easily manipulate their mark into doing what they want - often enabling them to find new marks once they are done with their current one. Due to their natural ease at infiltration and manipulation, they are often sent to the Material Realm by higher powers seeking to further their agendas.
