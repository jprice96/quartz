The origins of vampires are unclear - although most everyone knows that the way one becomes a vampire is by being exsanguinated by an existing vampire. how the original vampires came to be is unclear. It is believed by academics that they have existed since before the [[Culture/History/Timeline of the Material Realm#Age of Mortals|Age of Mortals]], and it has been speculated that through profane rituals one can transform oneself into a vampire in a similar way to becoming a lich, but the details are not known. What is known is many of the strengths and weaknesses of a vampire. Undead in nature, most vampires appear effectively indistinguishable from how they did in life, but they do not age nor do they succumb to most mundane diseases or ailments. Should they become injured, they heal rapidly, with only two known ways to truly kill them.

Foremost is sunlight - vampires will avoid it at all costs, as it quickly debilitates and destroys them. As a result, vampires take great care to sequester themselves away from the sun during daytime hours, mostly operating at night. Powerful vampires often are described as having many supernatural powers, such as taking on the form of beasts or even dark vapor, making them incredibly elusive and likely to just flee should they be forced into sunlight. The more dramatic means by which to kill a vampire involves driving a wooden stake through their heart before decapitating them, a practice confirmed by eulogists of the [[Culture/Factions/Whitesun Lodge|Whitesun Lodge]] but also a feat they urge be left to the professionals. When it comes to self-defence. the Lodge recommends the use of sanctified charms or an excess of [[Culture/Herbs and Foraging#Garrlick|garrlick]] to discourage them.  It is also common belief that a vampire cannot willingly enter a sacred site or cross over running water. All such tricks are not perfect however, the Lodge warns that vampires - especially more powerful vampires - can overcome such obstacles if driven to do so.

While the Lodge treats vampires as nothing more than a particularly insidious strain of undeath, there are some communities and cults that seek what they see as either a path to immortality or a sacred transformation. While their motives may be suspect to many, they have been known to provide additional information about vampirism to those who seek it - provided they believe you do not intend to use it against them. It is known that they do not require food or drink as mortals do, instead feeding upon the lifeblood of mortals. They also appear to have a preternatural charisma, allowing them to more easily hide amongst mortal communities. Beyond that, there are lists of various abilities and legends of vampires that seems to indicate that vampires not only retain the capabilities of their living selves, but also seem to gain newfound individual abilities in death.
