The common boar roams the forests and foothills of most of the continent, although it is more populous south of the [[Geography/Natural/Waterways#Reden|Reden]]. Omnivorous scavengers, they are hunted for meat and occasionally sport, despite their tenacity and ferocity when challenged.

## Giant Boar
While some think the giant boar to simply be a common boar grown to monstrous proportions, knowledgeable hunters and druids know that they are in fact another species. While still omnivorous, the giant boars' size both allows and necessitates hunting smaller beasts for meat. Mostly found in more isolated regions around the [[Geography/Natural/Forests#Black Forest|Black]] and [[Geography/Natural/Forests#Grey Forest|Grey Forests]] of the south, when they do wander close to settled areas they often become a notable threat to those living there. 

## Blood Boar
Born of twisted magics, a blood boar was birthed from a common boar in a ritual of blood and gore. Often dominated magically by those who created them, they are violent and have a preternatural ability to sense blood. Once they have scented a target's blood, they can run them down over long distances. Those that manage to break free of their maker's control often go feral and become a menace to any who come close.
